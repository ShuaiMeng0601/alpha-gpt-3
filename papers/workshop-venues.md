## Archival vs non-archival — what it means for a WIP you may extend

- **Archival** (ICAIF main track, FinNLP@EMNLP, EuroGP, GECCO, AAAI main track): gets a DOI / proceedings entry — a real, citable publication now. But you can typically hold **only one** archival home for the same work, and **ICAIF's main track explicitly bans** submitting work that is simultaneously under review at / published in another archival venue. So: pick **one** archival target; don't run ICAIF main + FinNLP + EuroGP on the same paper at the same time.
- **Non-archival** (NeurIPS / ICLR / AAAI / ICAIF workshops): no proceedings DOI. You get the talk, the feedback, and a CV line, **and keep the right to publish a full archival version later**. These do **not** trip ICAIF's dual-submission ban — so an archival ICAIF main-track submission **plus** a non-archival NeurIPS workshop submission is generally compatible (still verify each workshop's own policy).
- **For a work-in-progress you intend to grow** (your case — planned ablations, broader universe), the strategically clean path is: **a non-archival workshop now** (NeurIPS / ICAIF workshop) to get feedback and visibility, **then** an archival full paper (ICAIF main, a journal, or EuroGP/GECCO) once the ablations are in. If you want the citable line *immediately* and the work is ready, **ICAIF main track** is the one to spend your single archival shot on.

- **ICAIF main-track buffer is thin** (~2 days after an end-July draft; the AOE cutoff gives a little more). Have a fallback (ICAIF workshop / NeurIPS workshop) ready in case the draft slips.
