==============================================================================
 ALPHA-GPT — TERMINAL (FEATURE) REFERENCE
==============================================================================

A "terminal" is one input FEATURE: a date x PERMNO panel of values stored as
data/panels/<name>.parquet. Alpha expressions combine terminals with operators
(ts_mean, cs_rank, sub, ...) to produce a signal. forward_returns is the LABEL
(next-period return), not a terminal.

Currently on disk: 55 panels  ->  54 candidate terminals (forward_returns excluded).
Each run, terminal_selection keeps a stable core + the highest-|IC| extras up to
config.max_terminals = 40 (scored on the TRAIN split only, no look-ahead). With
only 54 candidates today, the 40-cap is barely binding (drops ~14).

NOT terminals: forward_returns (label) | data/aux/sector.parquet (neutralization)
             | data/universe/membership.parquet (point-in-time universe grid)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT'S MISSING  (would come from OSAP — not yet ingested)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The current set is almost entirely FUNDAMENTAL/ACCOUNTING + raw price/volume.
There are NO price-based/behavioral anomaly terminals: momentum, short-term
reversal, idiosyncratic volatility, 52-week high, liquidity (Amihud), share
issuance, analyst signals, seasonality. (e.g. the Momentum debate persona must
build momentum from `close` via ts_returns, because no momentum feature exists.)

Ingesting OSAP (openassetpricing.com -> scripts/ingest_osap.py) would add
~100-200 osap_* terminals covering exactly those missing families and push the
candidate pool from 54 to ~150-250.
